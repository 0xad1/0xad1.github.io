Insurance marketing often begins at the end of the story: risk, loss, a family protected. The stakes are real, but this language asks a young customer to identify with a future version of themselves they have not had much reason to picture.

## The missing middle

The better starting point may be the life already in motion: moving cities, first salaries, borrowed cars, parents who are becoming older. These are not fear hooks. They are signals that responsibility is quietly changing shape.

> Relevance is rarely created by shouting louder. It comes from finding the moment a category enters someone’s life.

The important change is not a younger colour palette or a more casual headline. It is building the conversation around the customer’s actual context, then offering enough clarity to make the next step feel manageable.
