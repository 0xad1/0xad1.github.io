### Field note 01 — Familiarity is usually an outcome

When a product feels inevitable, it is tempting to assume the insight was simply better. More often, the feeling is assembled: a clear problem, distribution that reaches the right moment, a social proof loop, and language that removes unnecessary effort.

### Field note 02 — The pitch is rarely the whole story

An elegant pitch can make an unfamiliar product understandable. It cannot make the product belong in a person's routine. The gap between understanding and habit is where this thread is spending most of its time.
