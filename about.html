I’m Aditya Singh. I have moved from software engineering into marketing, and I am currently pursuing an MBA in Marketing at NMIMS Mumbai.

ThinkInPark is a place to make that overlap visible: brands and systems, data and behaviour, technology and the much messier business of people deciding what matters to them.

I am especially interested in the work of making complex things understandable without making them dull. This is where I leave the observations, questions, and small projects that grow out of that interest.

Exploring startup use cases in India, customer trust in financial products, and the shape of good positioning.
