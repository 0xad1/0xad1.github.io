# ThinkInPark

## Project

ThinkInPark is Aditya's personal website and writing platform.

Domain:
https://thinkinpark.in

The site is primarily a personal intellectual space, not a conventional portfolio.

## Core idea

ThinkInPark should feel like a place to wander through ideas.

The site covers:

- Marketing
- Brands
- Consumer behaviour
- Business
- Startups
- Technology
- Analytics
- Personal observations
- Experiments

## Design philosophy

Minimal, editorial, intelligent and slightly playful.

Do NOT make the site look like:

- A corporate consulting website
- A generic developer portfolio
- A SaaS landing page
- A conventional blog template

The experience should feel closer to:

- An editorial magazine
- A personal notebook
- An intellectual playground

## Brand

Primary brand:

ThinkInPark

Possible tagline:

"I notice things. Then I write about them."

The Linkin Park inspiration is an Easter egg, not the visual identity.

Do not imitate Linkin Park's logo, typography, artwork or visual identity.

## Information architecture

Primary sections:

- Walks
- Bench
- Rabbit Holes
- Experiments
- About

### Walks

Long-form articles and essays.

### Bench

Short thoughts and observations that don't need to become full articles.

### Rabbit Holes

Topics currently being explored.

### Experiments

Projects, analyses, prototypes and things being built.

### About

Personal background and resume.

## UX principles

The website should encourage exploration.

Possible interactions:

- "Take me somewhere" random article button
- Idea map
- Related articles
- Current interests
- Reading length indicators
- Smooth but restrained transitions

Avoid excessive animations.

## Writing

Tone should be:

- Curious
- Analytical
- Conversational
- Intelligent
- Occasionally playful

Avoid corporate buzzwords.

## Design

Prioritize:

- Typography
- White space
- Editorial hierarchy
- Fast loading
- Mobile responsiveness
- Accessibility

Avoid:

- Excessive cards
- Gradient-heavy SaaS aesthetics
- Unnecessary animations
- Generic icons
- Stock imagery

## Important

Before making major structural changes:

1. Inspect the existing codebase.
2. Identify the framework and build system.
3. Preserve working functionality.
4. Make changes incrementally.
5. Run the site locally after changes.
6. Check responsive behaviour.
7. Explain significant architectural decisions.
